# Storybook Chronicles Website V2 Roadmap

**Artifact type:** Product roadmap  
**Status:** Active  
**Target milestone:** October 1, 2026

## Product Objective

Evolve Storybook Chronicles from a functional public Codex into a story-centered creative product while preserving the stable V1 foundation.

The roadmap uses an incremental delivery model:

**Stabilize → Design → Build → Validate → Release**

## Foundation Completed

- Stabilized Public Codex V1 before beginning major redesign work
- Verified anonymous and owner experiences
- Established the new primary navigation architecture
- Consolidated About, résumé, and contact planning
- Defined shared site-footer architecture
- Established V2 as an incremental replacement rather than a destructive rewrite

## Current Focus

### Strengthen the Skills Experience

Transform the Skills page from a capability list into an evidence-driven portfolio experience.

Current work includes:

- ClickUp project evidence
- Notion planning and architecture evidence
- Technical-delivery artifacts
- Creative-direction evidence
- QA and governance examples
- Responsive artifact viewers

## Story-Centered Product Experience

The next major product expansion reorganizes Storybook Chronicles around its four stories.

Planned experience:

**Storybook Chronicles → Choose Story → Enter Story Hub**

The first implementation prototype will focus on Story 1.

## Story 1 Hub

The Story Hub is planned as a connected exploration layer for:

- Characters
- Factions
- Locations

Desktop and mobile experiences will use different interaction patterns rather than forcing one layout across every device.

Accessibility and reduced-motion alternatives remain part of the design requirements.

## Power Architecture

Before redesigning the power interface, the underlying information model will be reviewed.

Planned hierarchy:

**Power Source / Category → Power System → Ability → Technique / Expression**

The system will also model how powers:

- Compete
- Counter
- Amplify
- Collaborate
- Merge
- Evolve

## Timeline Experience

Two complementary timeline modes are planned.

### Historical Timeline

A reference-focused chronological experience answering:

**What happened?**

### Saga Timeline

A more cinematic storytelling experience answering:

**Experience what happened.**

## Storybook Studio

Future media will be organized into one coherent creative experience rather than scattered navigation links.

Planned areas:

- Art
- Animation
- Music and composition
- Visual-development experiments

## Creator Control Room

The long-term Admin experience is planned as a creator-facing control system for:

- Publishing
- Content ordering
- Draft and review states
- Spoilers
- Media
- Story unlocks
- Timeline events
- Characters, factions, locations, and powers

Security and row-level access controls remain separate from editorial convenience.

## Mobile Creator Workflow

Routine Storybook management should eventually be possible away from the desktop.

### iPhone

Review, approve, edit, upload, publish, and check project work.

### iPad

Expanded editing, media management, and visual review.

### Desktop

Reserved for major development, infrastructure, migrations, debugging, and heavy asset work.

## Release Principles

- Preserve the stable V1 experience while V2 is built
- Replace features incrementally
- Do not delete underlying lore simply because presentation changes
- Validate desktop and mobile experiences
- Preserve spoiler protection
- Include accessibility and reduced-motion behavior
- Test anonymous, owner, and Admin experiences before release
